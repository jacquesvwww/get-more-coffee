How More Coffee works

Who we are, what makes the list, and how we make money, so you know exactly what you're looking at.

What More Coffee is

More Coffee lists live offers from independent specialty coffee roasters in England and Wales. The idea is simple: great coffee should be easier to say yes to. If there's a code, a sale or a subscription offer worth knowing about, you'll find it here, and you'll buy it straight from the roaster.

We don't sell coffee, take payments or hold stock. Every purchase happens on the roaster's own website, under their prices, delivery terms and returns policy.

What makes the list

  • Independent specialty roasters only. We choose every roaster by hand. Supermarket and commercial brands aren't included, however big the discount.
  • England and Wales, for now.
  • Real discounts. We leave out "was" prices that don't add up, such as the same old price on every bag size, a "was" price lower than today's price, or discounts too big to be believable.
  • Offers you can act on. A code, a reduced price or a clear way to claim it. Banner offers that can only be checked at checkout are left out for now.

This isn't a race to the cheapest coffee. We'd rather help you try a brilliant roaster for less than push you towards the lowest price per bag, which is also why we don't show price per gram.

How offers are found

A small program visits each roaster's public website about once a day and reads the offers shown there: banner codes, sale prices and subscription deals. It respects each site's robots.txt rules, keeps its visits light, and backs off if a site asks it to. Offers that end or pass their expiry date are removed automatically.

Always check the final price at the roaster's checkout. Offers can change or end at any time, and we can't promise every detail is correct. If something looks wrong, tell us at hello@getmore.coffee and we'll fix it.

How we make money

Right now, More Coffee doesn't make money. It's an independent side project. As it grows, we may earn money in these ways, and we'll always tell you when we do:

HowWhat it means for you
Affiliate linksSome links to roasters may earn us a small commission if you buy something. You pay exactly the same price. A commission never decides whether a deal is listed.
Featured or sponsored spotsA roaster may pay to have an offer shown more prominently. Paid placements will always carry a Sponsored label, and the offer itself must still meet the rules above.
Exclusive codesWe may agree codes with roasters that are only available through More Coffee. These are labelled too.

What we won't do: take money to hide a competitor's offer, make a discount look bigger than it is, or list roasters that aren't specialty to fill space.

Run a roastery?

If you'd like your roastery listed, want an offer corrected or removed, or want to talk about featured spots, email hello@getmore.coffee. Every outbound link carries utm_source=getmore.coffee, so you can see the visitors we send you in your own analytics.
