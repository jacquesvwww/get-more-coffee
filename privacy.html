Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The short version: you don't sign up and there are no advertising trackers. We use Google Analytics to see which pages and deals are useful, but only if you click "Accept"; if you don't, it never loads. Like almost every website, our hosting keeps basic technical logs, and if you email us we'll have your email. That's it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for marketing. We use a few service providers to run the site:

  • Vercel: website hosting (technical logs).
  • Google: Google Analytics (only if you accept), Google Fonts, and Gmail, where messages to hello@getmore.coffee arrive.
  • ImprovMX: forwards emails sent to @getmore.coffee to that inbox.
  • Supabase: our database. It holds information about roasters and their offers, not about visitors.

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards UK law allows, such as the UK's adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Technical logs are kept for a short period, as set by Vercel's log retention, and then deleted.
  • Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for 2 months, then deleted automatically. Totals in our reports, such as "120 visits last month", don't identify anyone.
  • Emails are kept for as long as we need them to deal with your message and any ongoing conversation, and are deleted within two years of our last contact unless we have a good reason to keep them, such as an ongoing agreement with a roaster.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you

  • delete it
  • restrict how we use it, or object to us using it

Email hello@getmore.coffee and we'll reply within one month. Usually there's no charge.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.
